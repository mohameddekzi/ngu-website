    About NGU

    A Center of Excellence in Higher Education

    New Generation University (NGU) was established in 2010 in Hargeisa, Somaliland, with the aim of providing high-quality higher education opportunities for the younger generation of Somaliland and beyond.

    The University's vision of becoming a Center of Excellence reflects its commitment to quality education, research, professional development, innovation, and community service.

    History

    Our Story

    NGU was established in response to the growing demand for quality private higher education and qualified professionals capable of contributing to national and regional development.

    2010 · Founded in Hargeisa

    New Generation University opens its doors to provide high-quality higher education opportunities for the younger generation of Somaliland and beyond.

    Growing academic offer

    Programs expand across seven faculties, from Social Sciences and Business to Engineering, Health Sciences, Agriculture and Islamic Studies.

    Graduate studies

    Fifteen master's programs serve graduates and professionals seeking advanced knowledge and leadership skills.

    Today

    A university committed to quality education, research, professional development, innovation, and community service.

    Our Vision

    A Center of Excellence

    To become a Center of Excellence committed to providing a holistic learning and teaching environment that produces qualified graduates who demonstrate professional competence, a sincere spirit of tolerance, and a strong sense of responsibility and commitment to society.

    Our Mission

    Education. Research. Service.

    Our mission is to provide quality higher education, research, and community services through superior and interdisciplinary academic programs that prepare undergraduate and graduate students with the professional knowledge, skills, and values needed to contribute meaningfully to society.
